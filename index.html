About Me

I am currently a Lecturer with the Faculty of Computer Science and Technology at Qilu University of Technology (Shandong Academy of Sciences). I received my Ph.D. degree from China University of Petroleum (East China), advised by Prof. Lianyong Qi ↗. My research focuses on recommender systems, with particular interests in sequential and point-of-interest recommendation, multimodal recommendation, large language models for recommendation, and trustworthy recommendation.
